# What "parked properly" means in the USA, and how the van meets it

| US rule (typical state code) | In the van | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Park on the right, with the flow of traffic; left side only on one-way streets | Bay finder searches the right-hand kerb; the left-hand skill exists (mirrored demos) for one-way streets and is not enabled in the stack | done / reserved |
| Parallel, within 18 in (0.46 m) of the kerb | Lidar slot's kerb gap 0.18 m → flank 0.45 m; arena slot = lane centre → flank 0.27 m; kerb distance now printed at every park | done 2026-09-05 |
| Wheels parallel to the kerb | Exams: heading off 0.2–2.6°; the arena's pass mark is "fully inside the 7 × 2.5 m slot" | done |
| Inside the space, not overhanging the next car | Occupancy bins along the kerb; 0.5 m end margins; the learned parker's feelers | done |
| No stopping in the traffic lane while waiting | Hold-short point when the chosen bay is taken; re-target ahead | done |
| Not within 15 ft of a hydrant, 20 ft of a crosswalk, 30 ft of a stop sign, or across a driveway | Not modelled: the simulator's town has no hydrants or driveways; the bay finder does not know crosswalks | gap |
| Tight space: pull alongside, reverse in | The round-9 instructor pulls past and reverses when the bay's approach is blocked or the forward swing no longer fits | taught in round 9 |
